# Product contract — Porthole

Target edition: **20 October 2026**. Porthole is the owned observability platform a US engineering organization buys to replace Datadog, New Relic, Grafana Cloud, Dynatrace or Splunk Observability, with the open instrumentation and investigation depth of Dash0, SigNoz, ClickStack and Honeycomb. Adjacent Resolve AI, Cleric and Traversal inform the investigation destination; they do not replace telemetry collection and storage. This is a new seed with no code or published contract: every clause is missing. Names and marks follow the desk’s NAME.md unchanged.

The job is to know whether production is healthy, why it is not, and what changed, before customers need to explain it. A buyer connects their estate, migrates live definitions and evidence, explores correlated signals, detects problems against SLOs, hands evidence to responders, approves bounded repairs, verifies recovery and keeps coverage and telemetry spend under control. An alert, diagnosis or silent chart alone does not finish that job.

## Scope and acceptance conventions

This is a complete category target, including owner installation, native mobile, migration, reporting and specialist performance capabilities. Shared Base code does not merge products. US region/USD and America/New_York organization reports are defaults, with viewer IANA zones and explicit DST offsets. The desk’s PORT-001–030 remain stable; PORT-028–030 are push-further clauses. Research and traceability are in [INVENTORY.md](../INVENTORY.md), [inventory.json](../inventory.json) and [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md). These research links are draft-workspace references; a delivered repository contract will retain its acceptance pair and identify the local research separately.

Every acceptance journey is future work through the running product’s actual browser, documented API/ingest endpoint, CLI or provider sandbox. Screen names here are required public doors, not claims of existing routes. Exercise every affected browser screen and success, empty, invalid, stale, unauthorized, unavailable and completed state at **1440 × 900 and 390 × 844**, with realistic multi-team US checkout, payments and fulfillment data; inspect keyboard/focus, readable graphs and tables, accessible names, contrast, reflow and persistent error feedback. Never hide permission enforcement in the UI. Native-client evidence uses real supported builds on devices or simulators.

Provider fakes are allowed only at the paid/network edge, built from the provider’s opened official wire schema; fixture source/version and request/response are inspectable. No mock application backend, fabricated telemetry, guessed provider acknowledgment, simulated production repair or generated benchmark establishes acceptance. Schema tests do not establish live partner credentials, native distribution or an executed compliance agreement. Dedicated authorized infrastructure supplies future capacity qualification; this drafting task runs no load on the shared Mac. Public-door tests exercise the product’s real storage and workers.

All latency/volume/accuracy numbers below are explicit target acceptance requirements, including the desk’s budgets, not measured claims. Declare resource envelope, workload, cache state, count/denominator, errors and percentile for qualification. Never lower scope because a provider, native app, protocol or analytical backend is difficult. Record and build the missing obligation.

## Target clauses

### Installation and custody

**PORT-031 — Owner-run Base installation.** An owner installs one isolated Porthole organization in their Cloudflare account through Base, with owned domain, storage, encryption keys, mail and explicitly selected analytical compute. Setup inventories prerequisites, US region, metering units and estimated USD infrastructure costs before provisioning. High-volume ingest, gRPC termination, eBPF and analytical workers may run on declared owner-authorized infrastructure, connected to the Base control plane with scoped identities; this is part of the product installation, not four databases the buyer must assemble. Installation, upgrade, export and recovery have supported documented commands. Missing bindings stop work and name the setting without exposing its value. **Acceptance evidence:** From a clean authorized Cloudflare account follow Base setup to a real sign-in, ingest stock OTLP and inspect a service. Break each required binding and verify startup and health diagnose it without secrets. Reinstall into a second account and prove data and authority isolation; inspect setup at 1440 and 390.

**PORT-032 — Sign-in and accountable membership.** Base provides owner bootstrap, secure email-link sign-in, sign-out and revocation, with SSO and MFA under PORT-025. Owners invite admins, maintainers, responders, read-only analysts, finance readers and restricted contractors. Removal revokes sessions, device access and delegated credentials immediately, and requires reviewed handover of owned services, monitors, reports and pending actions to an active member or explicit unassignment. Protect the last owner. Links are single-use, expiring and safe from mail-prefetch consumption; account enumeration is resisted. **Acceptance evidence:** Through setup, sign-in and Settings invite each role, consume a link once, expire another and sign out. Remove a maintainer with a running investigation and report, verify immediate API/mobile/MCP refusal and handover, and refuse removal of the last owner. Test forged links, CSRF and cross-organization access.

**PORT-033 — Durable audit with private contents.** Append a durable actor/time/action/result audit for membership, credentials, queries, exports, dashboards, monitors, SLOs, pipelines, retention, connector attempts, approvals and production actions. Store revision and opaque entity references, record failed privileged actions, and preserve occurrence and recording times. Query audit records scope and reproducibility without copying secrets or sensitive query literals into a less protected log. Owner export supports tamper detection and Evidence handoff. Actor erasure anonymizes identity while preserving attributable opaque action history. **Acceptance evidence:** Use UI and versioned API to run a query, refuse a restricted query, change a monitor, approve a repair and export audit. Restart, erase an actor and restore an archive; action order/results and opaque references survive and planted addresses, tokens and restricted literals do not leak.

**PORT-034 — Complete inactive archive and restore.** Owner exports the whole installation in a versioned streaming archive with manifest digests: configuration, service ownership, dashboards, notebooks, monitors/SLO definitions and history, telemetry or explicit durable object references, connectors, investigations, approvals, audit and erasure journal. Resume interrupted exports/restores; validate integrity and semantics before committing into an empty installation. Restore preserves source/history labels, pauses collection credentials, monitors, schedules, delivery and execution, interrupts leases and quarantines pending sends. Sessions, secret values and approval authority never transfer. Reauthorize each connection before resumption; corrupt or nonempty restore writes nothing. **Acceptance evidence:** Export a realistic multi-team estate with open alerts, customer-bucket history, a pending rollback and removed actors through Settings/CLI. Interrupt/resume, restore into another owner account, compare counts and samples, re-export, and prove no send, production action or live health occurs. Corrupt a chunk and try nonempty restore; both refuse without partial import.

**PORT-035 — Retention and operator-directed erasure.** Owners set retention by signal/stream/team and preview reductions, affected volumes and loss of historical analysis before a person approves deletion. Erase a selected service, user identifier or installation across indexes, derived caches, exports under product custody, jobs and customer-bucket objects when authorized. Show progress, provider refusals and backup retention limits; legal hold and bucket object lock remain explicit unresolved constraints. Retain minimal anonymized audit and a portable erasure journal so old archives accompanied by the later journal cannot resurrect erased data. Recompute derived views without portraying lost evidence as health. **Acceptance evidence:** Plant a US identifier in logs, traces, profiles, RUM and exports; run owner erasure via API, include a locked S3 object, restart and inspect searchable/storage remnants and the outstanding deletion. Restore an older archive with the later journal and prove no resurrection. A preview or unapproved retention change deletes nothing.

**PORT-036 — Agents are scoped credentials.** Every collector, CI job, integration and agent uses a named installation-local identity with signal/service/environment/action scopes, expiry, quotas, last use and immediate revocation. Read-only investigation cannot mutate monitors, drop telemetry, approve its own actions or acquire production credentials. A human grants bounded proposal/execution scopes separately. Secrets stay encrypted under owner custody and are excluded from prompts, logs, artifacts and portable archives. Treat telemetry, runbooks, code and tool output as untrusted data, never authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a checkout-only read key through Settings, query via REST/CLI/MCP and attempt another service, write, approval and credential minting; all unauthorized operations refuse server-side. Inject instructions into logs/runbooks, revoke during a job and verify no escalation or new read. Search prompts/audit/archive for planted secrets.

**PORT-037 — Recovery of observability itself.** Show independent control-plane, collector, ingest, queue, storage, query, monitor-evaluation and delivery health, with last accepted and queryable timestamps. A backend outage or stale telemetry produces Unknown or explicit incomplete coverage, never Healthy. Owner can back up, fail over analytical workers, resume durable work and restore the Base control plane using a documented recovery procedure; observability cannot depend solely on the backend being diagnosed for its own failure notification. **Acceptance evidence:** Stop the analytical backend and a collector during a firing monitor; read health and last-known data from a separate authenticated door, receive the configured independent failure notice and inspect Unknown. Restart with pending ingest/delivery and prove bounded backlog reconciliation, original timestamps and no duplicate repair execution.

**PORT-038 — US organization defaults and cost commitments.** Setup defaults to US English, a US data location and USD. Display bytes/GB, million spans/events and active-series units, retention, ingest/query/storage/egress/agent compute components and effective rate versions. Viewer timestamps use their IANA zone; organization reports default to America/New_York with explicit UTC offsets and daylight-saving transitions. Prices and capacity promises remain visible before acceptance; enabling an adapter or opening a screen cannot silently authorize new paid scope. A named person approves changed cost commitments. **Acceptance evidence:** Set up a US SaaS organization, inspect all cost units, change report zone and run periods across both New York DST transitions. Enable a new paid backend or agent budget and verify the owner sees exact incremental terms and must approve; unauthorized finance-only writes and silent upgrades refuse.
