# Product contract — Porthole

Target edition: **20 October 2026**. Buyer review 1 expands the destination with PORT-062–078 and additional acceptance for existing clauses; all earlier obligations and IDs remain. Round 2 sharpens full-traffic APM statistics and outcome-based gardening and adds the five agent jobs PORT-079–083. Round 3 adds PORT-084–087 for source-exact SLO types, qualified trace search, mandatory query translation and Loki/Pyroscope continuity, and sharpens related switching obligations. Round 4 adds PORT-088–090 for existing customer archives, metric backfill and durable trace evidence, and makes channel, notebook/view/link, Agent housekeeping and widget continuity explicit. Porthole is the owned observability platform a US engineering organization buys to replace Datadog, New Relic, Grafana Cloud, Dynatrace or Splunk Observability, with the open instrumentation and investigation depth of Dash0, SigNoz, ClickStack and Honeycomb. Adjacent Resolve AI, Cleric and Traversal inform the investigation destination; they do not replace telemetry collection and storage. This is a new seed with no code or published contract: every clause is missing. Names and marks follow the desk’s NAME.md unchanged.

The job is to know whether production is healthy, why it is not, and what changed, before customers need to explain it. A buyer connects their estate, migrates live definitions and evidence, explores correlated signals, detects problems against SLOs, hands evidence to responders, approves bounded repairs, verifies recovery and keeps coverage and telemetry spend under control. An alert, diagnosis or silent chart alone does not finish that job.

**PORT-032 — Sign-in and accountable membership: roles, links and removal.** Base provides owner bootstrap, secure email-link sign-in, sign-out and revocation, with SSO and MFA under PORT-025. Owners invite admins, maintainers, responders, read-only analysts, finance readers and restricted contractors. Removal revokes sessions and delegated credentials immediately. Protect the last owner. Links are single-use, expiring and safe from mail-prefetch consumption; account enumeration is resisted. Each member holds exactly one product role in an edition-owned record beside their Base account: owner and admin keep the Base roles of the same name, and the other five are Base members. Product policy only narrows Base policy and never replaces Base's active-session guard or final-owner protection. Owners manage every role; admins invite, change and remove only the five member-tier roles and never owners, admins, identity providers or domains. Maintainers manage services, dashboards, notebooks, monitors, routes, SLOs and pipelines and approve production actions within their scope; responders read, investigate, acknowledge and approve production actions within their scope but change no definitions; read-only analysts query and read within their scope; finance readers see USD usage, cost and budgets but no telemetry contents; restricted contractors read only explicitly listed services and environments and never export, share, approve, investigate, mint credentials or manage members. Nobody grants a role or scope wider than their own or changes their own role, an owner may step down only while another active owner remains, and membership authority is never delegated to a custom role, object grant or credential. The last active owner cannot be removed, deactivated, demoted, deprovisioned or erased. Owner and admin grants, removals and credential minting require a PORT-025 step-up within the preceding five minutes; revocation never waits for one. Every role, scope or membership change bumps a revision, so the next request on any door is refused or narrowed. Removal revokes the person's sessions, personal tokens and the read, proposal and execution credentials they hold custody of, which are never transferred; ingest credentials move to an active owner or admin custodian or are revoked in the same batch. **PORT-036 — Agents are scoped credentials: identity, grants and custody.** Every collector, CI job, integration and agent uses a named installation-local identity with signal/service/environment/action scopes, expiry, quotas, last use and immediate revocation. Read-only investigation cannot mutate monitors, drop telemetry, approve its own actions or acquire production credentials. A human grants bounded proposal/execution scopes separately. Secrets stay encrypted under owner custody and are excluded from logs. Each credential has one class: ingest, change-event, read, proposal or execution. Owners and admins mint ingest, change-event and read credentials no wider than their own live authority; only an owner mints a proposal or execution credential, these are separate credentials, and no role, custom role or credential can be delegated minting authority. Minting, re-scoping and rotation happen only in a native browser session after a PORT-025 step-up in the preceding five minutes; API, CLI and MCP doors refuse them. A secret is 256 random bits shown once and stored only as a SHA-256 hash with its prefix and last four characters, accepted only in an authorization header or the PORT-062 declared source header, and never logged. Expiry is required (read, ingest and change-event 365 days by default and 730 at most; proposal 30 and 90; execution 24 hours and 7 days), with rotation overlap of at most 7 days and none for execution. Quotas are required (600 requests a minute per credential by default, a daily scanned-bytes limit for reads and a daily USD budget for agents); exceeding one returns 429 with Retry-After and never queues silently. Effective authority on every request is the credential's scopes ∩ its custodian's live authority ∩ PORT-025 data scopes, rechecked with no cache inside the same transaction as any write. A read, proposal or execution credential is suspended permanently when its custodian is removed or narrowed and is never transferred; an ingest credential carries no read and moves only to an owner or admin custodian in the PORT-032 removal batch. No credential may approve, mint, change membership, roles, policies or retention, erase, export or restore the installation, read connector secrets or retire a source. Connector and provider secrets (PORT-055) are never returned by any door or given to a credential. Revocation is effective from the next request on every door. PORT-001, PORT-022, PORT-026, PORT-028, PORT-029, PORT-033, PORT-055, PORT-056, PORT-062, PORT-077, PORT-079, PORT-080, PORT-081, PORT-082 and PORT-083 inherit these classes before they ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a checkout-only read key through Settings and attempt credential minting with it; all unauthorized operations refuse server-side. Verify the secret is shown once and only its hash and last four characters are stored. Verify an admin cannot mint a proposal or execution credential, an owner cannot mint without a fresh step-up, and the API refuses minting. Verify a request over quota returns 429, last use updates, and the first request after revocation or custodian removal is refused while a reassigned ingest credential still authenticates. Search request logs for the planted secret.
